Empirical Analysis of Post-Training Quantization on Transformer Encoders

Dr. S. Ramanujan, Prof. M. Turing, Dr. H. Nyquist

Mathematical Formulation

Given a full-precision weight tensor $W \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{in} \times d_{out}}$, uniform affine quantization maps continuous real values to $b$-bit integer representations using a scale factor $s$ and zero-point offset $z$:

$$q = \text{clip}\left(\left\lfloor \frac{W}{s} \right\rceil + z, -2^{b-1}, 2^{b-1}-1\right)$$

where the dequantization function approximates the original tensor with quantization error $\epsilon$:

$$\hat{W} = s \cdot (q - z), \quad \epsilon = \|W - \hat{W}\|_F$$

Conclusion

Our findings establish that 4-bit activation-aware quantization achieves a 3.8x reduction in memory footprint with less than 0.36 degradation in perplexity, providing an attractive operating point for high-throughput deployment.
