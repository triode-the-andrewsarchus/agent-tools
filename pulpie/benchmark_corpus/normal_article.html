Understanding Modern Neural Network Architectures

By Elena Rostova • Published September 8, 2026

Over the past decade, deep learning models have evolved from convolutional networks to ubiquitous transformer architectures. In this article, we examine the structural innovations that made modern foundation models possible.

1. Attention Mechanisms and Efficiency

The core innovation behind transformers is self-attention, which computes pairwise token affinities across sequence lengths. However, standard self-attention scales quadratically with sequence length $O(N^2)$.

Recent architectural variants, including linear attention and state-space models (SSMs), address this bottleneck by maintaining constant memory overhead during inference while matching autoregressive quality on language modeling benchmarks.

2. Encoder vs. Decoder Trade-offs

While generative models typically employ decoder-only setups, encoder models remain exceptionally compute-efficient for classification, extraction, and embedding generation because they compute all token representations in parallel in a single forward pass.

This efficiency makes encoder models ideal for preprocessing pipelines where speed and structured retention are paramount.
